## Decision

Keep E005 as a feature family. Do not replace E004 with it.

Pooled RMSE improves by 0.0135, and the 732 non-zero rows improve by 0.0589 (0.782 to 0.723). That gain is in the tails: bin 1–2 RMSE 1.152 to 0.971, bin 4.5–5 RMSE 0.933 to 0.732. The middle gets worse: bin 2.5–3 0.571 to 0.605, bin 3.5–4 0.580 to 0.694. Zero-cohort RMSE is much worse (0.248 to 1.179) because those files are not silent; ASR still returns text, and word-count features do not mark them as 0.

OOF residual correlation with E004 is 0.52. The errors are related but not the same, so a later average is the justified next experiment. It was not run here.

E006 TF-IDF does not beat E004 (RMSE 1.061 vs 0.765) and does not beat E005. Word/bigram identity is a weak grammar signal on 769 transcripts. Do not carry TF-IDF forward unless a later error pass shows a specific n-gram failure E005 cannot see.
